## Tank Build Fundamentals: What Your Rig Needs

Every vehicle in Build and Kill Zombies is assembled from individual parts, and a tank-style build emphasizes durability, pushing power, and mounted firepower over raw speed. Think of your rig as four core systems:

| System | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Armor/Frame | Keeps the rig alive when zombies latch on | High |
| Engine | Determines speed and ramming force | High |
| Tank/Heavy Parts | Adds mass so smaller zombies bounce off | Medium–High |
| Weapons (Guns/Turrets) | Clear hordes before they damage the frame | High |

## Biome Progression Route for Tank Builds

Your tank build's effectiveness depends on matching your rig to the biome you're entering. Progression generally moves from grassy starter zones toward harsher territory, with the **snow biome** being a major mid-game milestone that community players celebrate reaching.

| Stage | Biome/Goal | Build Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starter zones | Small engine, basic frame, first weapon |
| 2 | Mid-distance zones | Bigger tank parts, second gun, armor plating |
| 3 | Snow biome | Full armor stack, mythic or high-tier gun |
| 4 | Boss zones | Maxed tank, coordinated team weapons |

Boss fights are the endgame check. Community gameplay shows players grouping up to "wreck that boss" once their rigs are fully upgraded — going in with a half-built vehicle usually ends with your frame getting shredded. If you're short on damage, bring a teammate: two mid-tier tanks consistently outperform one lonely maxed build against bosses, and you can revive or repair each other between waves.

### Tank vs. Speed Build: Which Should You Choose?

| Factor | Tank Build | Speed Build |
|---|---|---|
| Survivability | Excellent — hordes bounce off | Poor — one mistake ends the run |
| Early game | Slower start | Faster first milestones |
| Late game | Dominant in boss fights | Falls off against dense zones |
| Co-op value | Can carry teammates | Needs carrying |
| Skill floor | Low — forgiving | High — punishes errors |

For most players, especially anyone planning to team up with friends, the tank build is the safer long-term investment. Speed builds are fun for solo record-chasing, but they stall out once zombie density spikes.

## Pro Tips From the Community

- **Never let your rig "lag behind" the zone you're entering.** If you've reached a new milestone, your first purchase should close whatever gap caused damage on that run.
- **Test drive teammates' builds.** Popular YouTubers in this game trade rigs to see what works — borrowing a friend's setup is a free way to learn what parts you're missing.

- **Stack mass deliberately.** Zombies latching onto a heavy rig have less effect on handling than on light vehicles.
- **Pair a mythic gun with ramming.** Weapons soften hordes; your tank's mass finishes them. Together they keep your forward momentum intact.
