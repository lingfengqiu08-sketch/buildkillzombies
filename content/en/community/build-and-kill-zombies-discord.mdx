## Discord Etiquette: How to Be a Welcome Member

Large Roblox servers move fast, and a few simple habits go a long way:

- **Read the rules channel first.** Most servers require agreement before you can post anyway.
- **Check pinned messages** before asking questions — your answer is probably already there.
- **Use the right channel.** Code discussions belong in the codes channel, not general chat.
- **Never share personal information.** Standard advice, but especially important in servers with younger players.
- **Don't spam code requests.** If the dev hasn't dropped a new one, no amount of asking will speed things up.

Following these unwritten rules means faster answers, fewer mutes, and a better overall experience for everyone.
