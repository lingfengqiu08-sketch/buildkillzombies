## The Roblox Group Limit Problem

One important catch reported by players: **Roblox caps how many groups you can join**. If your account has hit the maximum number of groups, the "Join Group" button won't work until you free up a slot.

Here's how to manage it:

- Open your Roblox profile and navigate to your groups list
- Identify inactive or abandoned groups you no longer need
- Leave one or more groups to make room
- Return to the Build and Kill Zombies group page and join

If you're an active Roblox player with many group memberships, it's worth periodically auditing your list. Prioritize groups tied to games you play regularly, since group-gated rewards like this chest are increasingly common across the platform.
