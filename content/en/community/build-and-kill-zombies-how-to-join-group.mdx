## What Is Build and Kill Zombies?

Build and Kill Zombies is a Roblox survival game where your creativity is your lifeline. You construct vehicles — complete with engines, fuel tanks, weapons, and passenger seats — then drive them through waves of zombies to see how far you can go before the horde tears your ride apart.

The further your vehicle travels, the more cash you earn, which you reinvest into stronger builds. It's a loop that rewards both engineering smarts and quick upgrades, and community perks like the group chest accelerate that progression significantly.

| Core Game Loop | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Construct a vehicle from parts like engines, tanks, and seats |
| Drive | Push through zombie hordes as far as your build allows |
| Earn | Collect cash based on your best distance |
| Upgrade | Buy better parts and redeem codes for boosts |
| Repeat | Beat your distance record and survive longer each run |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I join the group in Build and Kill Zombies?

Leave your current game, visit the Build and Kill Zombies game page on Roblox, like the game, scroll down to the Developer section, click "View Community," and hit Join Group. Then return to the game to claim your free chest.

### Why can't I join the Build and Kill Zombies group?

The most common reason is hitting Roblox's group membership limit. Leave an inactive group from your profile, then try joining again.

### Is the group chest a daily reward in Build and Kill Zombies?

Community reports and player experience suggest the chest may refresh if you leave and rejoin the group, potentially making it a daily reward. This isn't officially confirmed, so treat it as a bonus rather than a guarantee.

### Do I need to like the game as well as join the group?

Yes. The chest requires both actions — liking the game and joining the community group. If you skip either step, the reward stays locked.
