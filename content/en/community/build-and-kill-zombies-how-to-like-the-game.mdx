### Common Roadblocks

- **Group limit reached**: Roblox caps how many groups you can join. If you're at the maximum, you'll need to leave an existing group before joining this one.
- **Chest reminder keeps appearing**: If the prompt won't go away, double-check that *both* the like and the group join registered — the game tracks them separately.
- **Don't leave the group after claiming**: Player experience suggests removing the like or leaving the group may stop future daily chests from appearing.
