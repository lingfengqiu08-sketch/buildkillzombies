## How Weapons Actually Work in Build and Kill Zombies

Every run follows the same short loop. You roll parts in the lobby, assemble your car on your plot, then drive it down the track into waves of zombies. Cash is paid out based on distance covered, so weapons aren't just for fun — clearing zombies faster keeps your car alive longer, which means more distance and more money per run.

A few mechanical facts shape every weapon decision:

- **Parts are rolled, not bought.** Two separate machines in the lobby handle part rolls, and weapons come through those rolls like everything else.
- **Fuel ends runs, not damage.** A run stops with an "Out of fuel" message, so even the best weapons can't save a build with a tiny tank.
- **The "New distance record" banner is your test.** If a weapon change works, you'll see that banner. If you don't, swap the setup.
- **Everything resets between runs except Skills.** The Skills tree is the only permanent progression, which is why it matters so much for weapon-focused builds.

## Skills That Make Your Weapons Hit Harder

The Skills menu is the only permanent progress in the game. Everything else resets between runs, so every cash investment here compounds across hundreds of future runs. Since everything in the game runs through part rolls — including the weapons you get — Luck is the confirmed top priority.

| Priority | Skill | Why It Matters for Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Luck I | Improves roll outcomes at both machines — better odds at rolling top-tier cannons |
| 2nd | Luck II | Stacks the Luck bonus; the roll improvement compounds |
| Later | Gold Roll I | Costs thousands of cash; a long-term goal for high-tier weapon rolls, not an early buy |

The logic is simple: you can't manually buy the best weapons, so improving your rolls is functionally the same as upgrading your arsenal. Player experience across the community consistently says Luck I → Luck II → everything else is the correct order.

## Rolling Parts: How to Actually Get Top-Tier Weapons

Since weapons arrive through the roll machines rather than a shop menu, your grinding strategy matters as much as your aim. Here's the practical loop:

1. **Redeem codes first.** Five working codes were live as of mid-September 2026 — COMMUNITY, CashDrop, MOREFRIENDS, HACKER, and 1x1x1x1. COMMUNITY alone grants 1K cash plus a small engine and small tank, a solid head start on your first rolls. To redeem: open SHOP, navigate to EXCLUSIVE SHOP, find COMMUNITY CODES, and hit REDEEM.
2. **Roll Engines and Fuels before weapons.** A cannon is useless with no fuel tank to support the run.
3. **Roll weapons second,** once your fuel economy supports longer tracks.
4. **Watch the boss timer.** The HUD shows a "NEXT BOSS IN: XX:XX" countdown, and boss events are shared across the whole server. Time your runs so you're back in the lobby when it fires — a cannon-heavy build positioned correctly can capitalize far better than a mid-run car.

For free-to-play grinders, patience is the strategy. For Robux spenders, community consensus points to the **permanent 4x Luck boost at 229 Robux** as the most defensible purchase in the Exclusive Shop, since Luck compounds across every roll you'll ever make — including every future weapon roll. Cash packs, by contrast, get spent and vanish.

## FAQ

### What are the Build and Kill Zombies best weapons?

The strongest confirmed setup per community reports is a hybrid build: one high-damage cannon for dense hordes paired with a rapid-fire mounted gun for lighter waves, mounted on the Blue Monster blueprint frame for maximum weapon slots. Early game, use whatever your rolls provide and prioritize a fuel setup that supports longer runs.

### Do weapons carry over between runs in Build and Kill Zombies?

No. Everything resets between runs except the Skills tree. That's why buying Luck I first is the standard advice — better rolls mean better weapons every single run, permanently.

### Which blueprint should I use for a cannon-heavy build?

Blue Monster. It's explicitly designed as the heavier option with more weapon slots, making it the natural home for the hybrid cannon setups that dominate late waves. Fast Furious suits speed-focused distance runs with lighter armament.

### How do I get cash fast to afford better weapons?

Redeem all five working codes (COMMUNITY, CashDrop, MOREFRIENDS, HACKER, 1x1x1x1), invest early cash into Luck skills, and roll Engines and Fuels before weapons so each run lasts longer and pays out more distance-based cash. Track your "New distance record" banners as proof each change is working.
