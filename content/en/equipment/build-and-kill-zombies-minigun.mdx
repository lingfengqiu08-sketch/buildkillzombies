## Why the Minigun Dominates Zombie Uprising

If you've ever been pinned against a wall by a hundred shambling bodies while your rifle clicks empty, you already understand why the Build and Kill Zombies minigun conversation keeps coming up. In Zombie Uprising, the Minigun is the closest thing to a portable swarm-delete button — a Tier 9 Heavy Machine Gun with the biggest magazine and largest ammo reserve of any weapon in the game.

But it's not a "buy it and win" weapon. The Minigun trades raw damage per shot for an absurd rate of fire, which means sloppy ammo management will leave you staring down a horde during a painfully long reload. This guide breaks down the numbers, the ideal loadout, and the habits that separate Minigun gods from Minigun snacks.

## Minigun Overview: Core Stats at a Glance

Per community wiki data and player experience, here's what you get for your $40,000:

| Stat | Value / Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon Class | Heavy Machine Gun (HMG) |
| Tier | 9 (one step below the Tier 10 M2HB) |
| Cost | $40,000 |
| RPM Rank | 4th highest of all weapons in-game |

The headline here is sustain. No other weapon lets you hold the trigger down as long while keeping a steady stream of lead flowing. Combined with zero range falloff, the Minigun rewards players who like to set up a lane and turn it into a bullet corridor.

## Strengths and Weaknesses: The Full Breakdown

Before you drop $40k, know exactly what you're buying into.

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| Blistering rate of fire for elite crowd control | Low damage per bullet — kills take longer |
| Massive 1,300-round magazine capacity | Long, punishing reload time |
| Huge 4,250-round reserve pool | No tactical reload option |
| No range penalty at any distance | High recoil and spread (fixable — see below) |
| Great for grinding assist points | Cannot equip Grips, Barrels, or Sides |

The damage-per-shot issue is the big one. Per player experience, the Minigun needs a lot of focused rounds to put down a single zombie, so headshot discipline matters more than it would on a hard-hitting battle rifle. The good news: bullet-type attachments like AP, RIP, and AP RIP meaningfully shore up the damage output.

## Best Minigun Loadout: Attachments and Perks

Since the Minigun locks out grip, barrel, and side attachments, your customization choices narrow to optics, magazines, and bullet types. Here's what the community recommends:

| Slot | Recommendation | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magazine | Speed Loader variants | Cuts the brutal reload time, your biggest vulnerability |
| Bullet Type | AP, AP RIP, or RIP | Directly fixes the low per-shot damage |
| Optic | Anything on the picatinny rail | The in-game model supports optics; pair with headshot aiming |
| View | Third Person | Nearly eliminates the recoil problem |
| Perks | Speed reload + ammo capacity perks | Unlocks the full 1,300/4,250 round ceiling |

The Speed Loader magazine deserves special emphasis. Because the Minigun has no tactical reload, every reload is a full one — and a careless reload mid-swarm is how Minigun users die. Stack speed reload perks with a Speed Loader mag and you turn a death sentence into a minor pause.

## How to Play the Minigun: Tips That Actually Work

The Minigun isn't a run-and-gun weapon. It's a lane-holder, a horde-shredder, and — per community reports — the best point-grinding tool in the game, since hitting large numbers of zombies at once racks up assists fast.

**Core tactics:**

- **Chug Bloxy Cola.** This utility boosts your rate of fire even higher. On a weapon that already sits 4th in RPM, the damage output scaling is significant.
- **Aim for headshots.** Low per-bullet damage means body shots waste ammo. Headshots stretch your enormous magazine even further.
- **Top off between waves.** Ammo drains at an alarming rate even with a massive reserve. Reload proactively, never reactively.
- **Use Third Person view.** Player experience consistently reports this nearly cancels the recoil issue — the Minigun essentially becomes "a beam of death."
- **Farm swarms, not singles.** Against lone zombies, swap to a secondary. The Minigun shines when the screen is full of targets.

## Is the Minigun Worth $40,000?

Compared to its HMG sibling, the Tier 10 M2HB, the Minigun wins on raw sustain and rate of fire, while the M2HB generally hits harder per shot. Your choice depends on playstyle:

| Factor | Minigun | M2HB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier | 9 | 10 |
| Rate of Fire | 4th highest in-game | Lower |
| Magazine Size | Largest in-game | Smaller |
| Damage per Shot | Low | Higher |
| Best For | Swarm control, assist farming | Picking off tougher targets |

For players grinding points or surviving endless swarms with a squad, the Minigun earns its price tag. If you're a precision player who takes careful shots, save for the M2HB instead.

## Fun Trivia About the In-Game Minigun

The weapon is modeled after the real-world General Electric M134 Minigun, which can fire up to 6,000 rounds per minute — roughly 100 rounds every second. The in-game model includes clever details like an external skeleton, a reversed pistol grip, a left-side carry handle, and a picatinny rail for optics. Notably, the spinning barrel no longer rotates when firing (it did at launch), and sharp-eyed players can spot the grey tube-shaped magazine tucked into the model.

Realistically, shouldering an 80-pound Minigun with no buttstock would wreck your spine — but as the wiki puts it, entertainment reasons excuse the physics here. The weapon was also originally classified as an LMG before an update reclassified it as a proper HMG.

## FAQ: Build and Kill Zombies Minigun Questions Answered

**How much does the Minigun cost in Zombie Uprising?**
The Minigun costs $40,000 and is a Tier 9 Heavy Machine Gun, one of only two HMGs in the game alongside the M2HB.

**What's the best Build and Kill Zombies minigun setup?**
Pair a Speed Loader magazine with AP or RIP bullets, reload-focused perks, and Third Person view. Add Bloxy Cola mid-fight for maximum rate of fire and always aim for headshots.

**Why does my Minigun run out of ammo so fast?**
The high RPM drains even the game's largest reserve quickly. Per community reports, the fix is topping off between waves and using capacity perks to hit the full 1,300-round magazine and 4,250-round reserve.

**Is the Minigun or M2HB better for grinding?**
For grinding points, the Minigun is generally superior — its rate of fire and massive magazine let you damage huge swarms at once, farming assists continuously. The M2HB is better for higher per-shot damage against individual tough targets.
