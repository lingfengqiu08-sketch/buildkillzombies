### Quick Loadout Tips

- **Don't neglect placement**: Even an S-tier turret underperforms with bad positioning. Put high-range turrets in the center of your base and short-range options like the Flamethrower at chokepoints.
- **Sell and rebuild freely**: Most Roblox tower-defense style games let you recoup some cost from sold turrets. Don't be afraid to replace early-game equipment when you can afford better.
- **Balance slow and fast turrets**: The Frost turret's C-tier ranking doesn't mean it's useless — slowing enemies so your Gatling can shred them is a legitimate strategy, just not a complete one.
