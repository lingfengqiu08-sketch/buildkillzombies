## How Weapons Work in Build and Kill Zombies

Weapons in this game aren't purchased from a traditional armory. Instead, you **roll** for them, similar to gacha-style RNG systems in other Roblox titles. Each roll can land you a car part or a weapon of varying rarity, and you attach whatever you win directly onto your vehicle's frame.

Once mounted, weapons fire automatically as you drive through zombie crowds, meaning your build path matters more than twitch aim. Community reports suggest that players who stack multiple weapon types early on see far better distance runs than those relying on a single gun.

A few key principles from player experience:

- **Placement matters.** Weapons mounted toward the front of your car tend to engage zombies earlier, clearing a path before your bumper makes contact.
- **Coverage beats raw damage.** Two mid-tier weapons covering both sides of the road often outperform one high-rarity weapon with a narrow firing arc.
- **Distance milestones gate rewards.** Some free code rewards require a best distance of 1,000 or 2,500 studs, so a balanced build helps you hit those thresholds faster.

## How to Redeem Codes for Free Weapons and Cash

Codes are the fastest way to fund your weapon upgrades without hours of grinding. The developer announces new codes primarily through the game's **official Discord server**, but many are also shared by major outlets like Eurogamer, which maintains an updated codes list for the game.

Here's the redemption process, step by step:

1. **Launch Build and Kill Zombies** in Roblox.
2. **Complete the tutorial** if you haven't already — the code menu is locked until you do.
3. Click the yellow **SHOP** button on the left side of your screen.
4. **Scroll to the bottom** of the shop menu that pops up.
5. Type or paste your code into the field and hit **REDEEM**.

| Redemption Step | Common Mistake | Fix |

| Completing the tutorial | Skipping it and wondering why codes fail | Finish the tutorial fully first |

That last row is important: not every code works immediately. Several current rewards are gated behind **best distance milestones** — one requires a best distance of 1,000 studs, another requires 2,500. If a code "fails," check whether it's actually telling you that you haven't unlocked it yet.

## FAQ

**How do I get better weapons in Build and Kill Zombies?**
You obtain weapons through in-game rolls and through code redemption. Redeeming active codes is the fastest route — one current code grants a Crossbow and Mounted Gun along with 1,000 Cash, while Hacker Tokens from codes fund premium rolls for rarer weapons.

**Do weapon upgrades stack with skill tree bonuses?**
Yes. The skill tree provides account-wide or run-wide boosts that stack on top of your mounted weapons' base damage. Community players recommend investing skill points in weapon damage first for the biggest early gains.

**Why won't my Build and Kill Zombies code work?**
Three common reasons: you haven't completed the tutorial, the code has expired, or it's gated behind a best distance requirement (1,000 or 2,500 studs). Double-check the requirements and make sure you're typing the code into the field at the bottom of the SHOP menu.

**What are Hacker Tokens used for?**
Hacker Tokens are the game's premium currency for rolls, letting you chase rarer car parts and weapons for your build. Codes regularly hand out 50–150 Tokens, so redeeming them consistently is the most reliable way to stockpile them without spending Robux.
