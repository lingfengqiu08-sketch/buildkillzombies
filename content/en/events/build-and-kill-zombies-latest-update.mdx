## The Lightweight Zeus Slayer Build (Step by Step)

Community reports and player-shared tutorials describe a remarkably simple counter-build for Zeus that requires almost no protective equipment. Here's the breakdown:

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | Place wheels and make the chassis four blocks long | Creates a compact, agile frame |
| 3 | Mount the driver seat at the rear | Keeps you safer from frontal boss attacks |
| 4 | Install two of your best engines | Enough power without excess fuel drain |
| 5 | Add your best available fuel | Maximizes uptime during the fight |
| 6 | Equip bullet-type weapons for DPS | Bullets reach the boss and its minion troops earlier, boosting effective damage output |

That's it. No blocks, no protectors, no heavy armor. According to players who've tested this approach, the entire build takes just a couple of minutes to assemble.

### The Logic Behind Each Choice

- **Light chassis = better dodging.** Zeus's attacks are dodgeable, and a featherweight car turns far more responsively than an armored one.
- **Two engines, not four.** Fuel consumption stays low, meaning longer fights without refueling mid-encounter.
- **Bullet-type weapons.** Because projectiles engage the boss and its escorting troops at greater range, you accumulate a bigger share of total damage — which translates directly into faster token payouts.
- **Rear-mounted seat.** A small quality-of-life touch that reduces the chance of you personally getting clipped by an attack sweep.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with the right build, players frequently sabotage their own farming runs. Watch out for these pitfalls:

- **Over-arming the build.** Adding "just one more weapon" adds weight, which kills the maneuverability advantage that makes this strategy work.
- **Skipping the vehicle save.** Rebuilding from scratch every session wastes minutes you could spend farming.
- **Ignoring minion troops.** Bullet-type weapons help clear the boss's escorts, but driving into a packed crowd still costs you speed and control.
- **Bringing tank builds to a boss fight.** Heavy armor feels safe, but against Zeus it just makes you a slow, predictable target.
