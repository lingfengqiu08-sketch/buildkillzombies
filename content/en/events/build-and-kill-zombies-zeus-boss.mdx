The Zeus boss fight is the centerpiece of this update. While the developers haven't published an official difficulty breakdown, community reports and player experience from the first days of the event suggest a few consistent patterns:

## Armored Parts, Titan Wheel, and the New Weight System

Update 3 doesn't just add weapons — it fundamentally changes how building works in Build and Kill Zombies.

### Armored Parts

Armored parts are tougher base components designed to survive heavier punishment. Given that the Zeus boss fight is the marquee content, these parts exist to help your defenses withstand boss-level attacks and elite zombie waves during the event.

### The Titan Wheel

The Titan Wheel is a massive new wheel part. Player experience from the first days of the event suggests it's aimed at large vehicle builds and heavy mobile bases — which pairs perfectly with the next feature.

### Build Weight Stats

This is the biggest systemic change. Builds now have weight stats, meaning every part you place contributes to your total build weight. Heavier builds likely trade mobility or speed for durability. This creates real build-craft decisions:

- **Light builds** — faster, more agile, but fragile against Zeus-tier attacks
- **Armored builds** — tanky and boss-ready, but potentially slower with standard wheels
- **Titan Wheel builds** — heavy constructions that stay mobile thanks to the new oversized wheels

The developers also confirmed that **turbo now considers direction**, meaning turbo boost behaves more predictably based on which way your build is facing. For players who've wrestled with janky vehicle physics, this is one of the most requested-style fixes you could ask for.
