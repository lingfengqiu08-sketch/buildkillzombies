## What Is Build and Kill Zombies?

**Build and Kill Zombies** is a Roblox zombie-hunting experience where the entire game revolves around one core loop: roll for parts, bolt them onto a vehicle chassis, and drive your creation into waves of the undead to earn Cash. This Build and Kill Zombies beginner guide walks you through everything you need to know — from redeeming codes and farming the right parts to building a tanky boss-killer that can survive late-game fights.

The beauty of the game is that building optimally matters far more than driving optimally. You can weave through gaps in the horde, jump ramps, or plow straight into zombies — the game rewards smart construction over fancy steering. That means every Cash you spend should push your build forward, not vanish into random rolls you'll never use.

Here's the basic gameplay loop at a glance:

| Step | Action | Goal |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Roll for parts | Unlock new blocks and weapons |
| 2 | Build in the Build menu | Attach parts to your chassis |
| 3 | Drive into the field (F) | Kill zombies and earn Cash |
| 4 | Upgrade skill tree | Boost long-term progression |
| 5 | Repeat and refine | Cross multiple zones with ease |

## Controls You Need to Know

Before anything else, get comfortable with the default control scheme. It's simple, and you'll use it hundreds of times:

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Movement | W, S, A, D |
| Jump | Spacebar |
| Interact | E |
| Drive / Return to base | F |

That last one — pressing F — is your lifeline. It lets you jump into your vehicle and, just as importantly, return to base instantly when you need to rebuild or resupply.

## Step 1: Redeem Codes Before Anything Else

The very first thing any new player should do is claim the active codes. According to community reports, codes give you free resources that make the early Cash grind dramatically easier — and they take less than a minute to redeem.

**How to redeem codes:**

1. Open the **Shop**
2. Scroll all the way down to the code entry area
3. Type in a code and confirm

## Step 2: Farm Parts Smartly (Don't Waste Cash)

The single biggest mistake new players make, based on player experience, is blowing all their Cash on random rolls. Random rolls feel exciting, but targeted rolls are what actually build a strong vehicle.

### Use "Select Category" for Targeted Rolls

Before rolling, open the **Select Category** option and confirm a specific category — for example, blocks. Every subsequent roll will then pull only from that category. This makes farming a specific build piece vastly more efficient than gambling on the full pool.

### Understand Part Rarities

Parts come in multiple rarity tiers. The higher the rarity, the more powerful the part:

| Rarity | Power Level |
|--------|-------------|
| Common | Baseline |
| Uncommon | Slight upgrade |
| Rare | Solid mid-game option |
| Epic | Strong |
| Legendary | Excellent |
| Mythic | Top-tier |

Track everything you've unlocked in the **Index**, which lists each part and its stats. It's your best reference when deciding whether a new roll is actually an upgrade.

## Step 3: Learn the Building Rules

Vehicle construction in Build and Kill Zombies has a few hidden rules that drastically affect performance. Master these early and you'll avoid painful rebuilds later.

### Weight Matters

Every block adds weight, and heavy vehicles handle worse. The weight hierarchy, per community testing, looks like this:

| Block | Weight |
|-------|--------|
| Glass | Lightest |
| Basic blocks | Medium |
| Carbon fiber | Heaviest |

If your build starts feeling sluggish, swapping some panels to glass can shave meaningful weight without sacrificing much.

### Block and Support Limits

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| Max block count | 250 blocks |
| Support item limit | Applies mainly to weapons that shoot |
| Exempt from limit | Fuel, engines, and normal blocks |
| Doesn't count | Shredder-type items |

In practical terms: you can load up freely on structural and utility parts, but be deliberate about how many shooting items you mount. Every weapon slot counts.

## Step 4: Aim for the Best Weapons

When your build starts getting serious, two weapons stand out above the rest, according to community reports:

| Rank | Weapon | Why It's Great |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Electro Glitch | Strongest option currently listed |
| 2 | Mounted Gun | Close second, easier to fit |

Both are worth chasing once you have a stable chassis. In the meantime, don't overlook passive damage: spikes and spike tires mow down zombies just by driving through them, and they don't compete with your weapon slots.

## Step 5: Upgrade Your Skill Tree

The Skills menu is easy to ignore early on, but experienced players agree it becomes increasingly important the deeper you go. Every upgrade compounds over time, so spend points consistently rather than hoarding them. Treat skill upgrades as a parallel progression track to your vehicle — the strongest builds in the game come from players who leveled both.

## Step 6: Farm Hacker Parts While the Event Lasts

Once you hit mid-game, **hacker parts** should become a major priority. They're extremely strong on advanced vehicles and especially valuable in boss fights.

Here's the catch: the hacker boss isn't started from a location on the map. Instead, you enter through the **timer on the right side of your screen**. Many newer players never realize this and miss the event entirely.

Because community reports suggest the hacker event may not remain available forever, your strategy should be:

- Farm it aggressively while it's active
- Stack as many hacker items as possible
- If the event disappears later, fall back on your strongest support items instead

Think of it like a limited-time resource in any live-service game — the players who grind early reap the rewards for months.

## Step 7: Build a Tanky Vehicle for Boss Fights

For boss fights, durability beats raw damage. A fragile glass cannon might clear normal zones quickly, but it will crumble in extended boss encounters. A proven tanky setup, per player experience:

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Spikes | Passive frontline damage |
| Spike tires | Run over zombies while staying protected |
| Strong weapons mounted on top | Sustained firepower from a safe height |
| Heavy armor blocks | Survive long, hard fights |

One more quality-of-life tip: if you have the right parts, you can use the available spawn spots that let you deploy **two builds**. That makes swapping between a fast zombie-farming car and a heavy boss-fighting rig instant — no dismantling required.

## FAQ

### What is Build and Kill Zombies about?

Build and Kill Zombies is a Roblox game where you build a vehicle powerful enough to drive across the field and destroy zombie hordes, earning Cash to roll for better parts and grow stronger.

### Is Build and Kill Zombies beginner-friendly?

Yes. A solid Build and Kill Zombies beginner guide strategy is simple: redeem codes, roll by category, and spend Cash only on upgrades that directly strengthen your build. The learning curve is gentle, and co-op lets you team up with others on the same server.

### Can I play Build and Kill Zombies with friends?

Absolutely. The built-in co-op feature lets players on the same server build vehicles together or cruise the field side by side.

### Is the game free to play?

Yes — Build and Kill Zombies is completely free to play on Roblox with no required premium purchases.
