## Basic Movement and Camera Controls

Like most Roblox experiences, Build and Kill Zombies uses the standard control scheme, which means you can jump straight in without relearning the basics. Once your vehicle is built and spawned, you'll take the wheel and drive through zombie waves, so movement mastery matters just as much as building speed.

| Action | Default Control |
|---|---|
| Move character / drive vehicle | W, A, S, D |
| Jump | Spacebar |
| Sprint | Left Shift (player experience varies) |
| Rotate camera | Right mouse button (hold and drag) |
| Zoom in/out | Mouse scroll wheel |
| Interact / select | Left mouse button (click) |
| Open chat | / (forward slash) |
| Open Roblox menu | Esc |

A few practical notes from community reports:

- **Camera zoom is your best friend.** Pull back while building to see your whole chassis at once — the max block count is 250, and it's easy to lose track of your layout at close range.
- **Drive with momentum.** Zombie hordes reward speed. Hesitating mid-drive is how builds get shredded.

## Building Controls: Placing, Rotating, and Removing Parts

The real "controls" in Build and Kill Zombies are the building tools. Your vehicle is your survival tool, and knowing how to manipulate parts quickly is what separates early-game scrap heaps from boss-killing tanks.

| Building Action | How to Do It |
|---|---|
| Select a part | Click it in your inventory or build menu |
| Place a part | Click on your build platform |
| Rotate a part | Press R before placing (standard Roblox building convention) |
| Delete a part | Click the part and select the delete option |
| Confirm a build | Use the confirm/roll button in the build menu |

Here are the building rules you should internalize before your first serious build:

- **Block weight matters.** Glass is the lightest block, followed by basic blocks and carbon fiber. Lighter builds accelerate faster; heavier builds survive longer. Choose based on your playstyle.
- **You have a 250-block cap.** According to player-shared info, a future update may raise this maximum, but for now every block counts.
- **Fuel, engines, and weapons are unlimited.** You can place as many of these as you want. The exception is support items, which only count toward items that fire — things like the shredder don't add to that limit.
- **The best weapon right now is the Electro Glitch**, followed by the mounted gun. Prioritize these when rolling for parts.

## Menu Controls: Rolling for Parts and Category Filtering

One of the most underused "controls" in the game isn't a keybind at all — it's the part-selection menu. Beginners often roll randomly and waste chances on parts they don't need. Here's the smarter approach:

1. Open the **Select Category** menu.
2. Choose the part type you want (for example, blocks).
3. Confirm your selection.
4. Roll.

Now every roll only gives you that category. If you're hunting engines or weapons, this filter dramatically improves your odds of getting the parts your build actually needs. It's a simple menu interaction, but per community guides, it's one of the biggest early-game time-savers.

You can also spawn in two pre-made builds directly if you have the required parts — a great shortcut while you're still learning the building controls.

## FAQ

**What are the default Build and Kill Zombies controls?**
The game uses standard Roblox controls: WASD for movement and driving, Spacebar to jump, the mouse for camera control and part placement, and R to rotate parts while building. The building menu, shop, and code redemption are all accessed through on-screen menus via left-click.

**How do I rotate parts when building my vehicle?**
Select your part, press R to cycle its rotation, then click to place it. Rotating matters a lot for spike placement and weapon angles on tank builds.

**What is the block limit in Build and Kill Zombies?**
The current cap is 250 blocks. Fuel, engines, and weapons are exempt from the limit, while support items only count for items that fire. Community reports suggest the cap may increase in a future update.

**Are there controls for entering the hacker boss fight?**
Yes — instead of using the map, watch the timer on the right side of your screen and enter through it when the hacker event is live. Bringing hacker parts into boss fights is currently the strongest strategy in the game.
