## Why Distance Equals Money in Build and Kill Zombies

Every run in *Build and Kill Zombies* ends in one of two ways: zombies destroy your ride, or you run out of fuel. Your reward is calculated based on the distance you covered, measured in studs. That means earning money fast isn't about grinding more runs — it's about making each run last longer.

Think of your car as a fuel-efficiency puzzle. Every extra part adds weight, and weight burns fuel faster. Players in the community consistently report that the sweet spot is a build that's roughly **80% fuel capacity and 20% engine power**. This ratio keeps fuel consumption low while maintaining a balanced, steady speed — exactly what you want for a long haul.

| Fuel capacity | Extends total run length |

### Fuel and Engine Placement (The Most Important Part)

Here's where most players go wrong. The community consensus is clear:

- Put your **best fuel tank at the front**, not the back.
- Use only **two engines total**, with your best engine at the front. Fewer, higher-quality engines reduce weight and cut wasted fuel consumption.
- Fill the remaining space with fuel — spam it. The target ratio is about **80% gas, 20% engine**.

Why the front? Zombies attack the rear first, and when rear-mounted fuel barrels get destroyed, your fuel drains faster. Keeping your best tank up front means it survives longer. As a bonus, once the rear of the car gets chewed off, the reduced weight actually makes your handling more precise and your speed increases — the game's balance system keeps the build stable even as parts fall away.

## Driver Seat and Weapon Placement Tips

Two placement decisions can make or break a long run:

**Driver seat — go front.** Zombies swarm the back of the vehicle first. If your driver seat is rear-mounted, it gets destroyed while the rest of your car is still intact, ending your run prematurely. Front placement keeps you alive as long as possible.

**Weapons — front and middle.** Mount all guns at the front of the build, positioned in the middle vertically. Community players warn that weapons placed underneath the chassis can cause the car to get stuck when you start driving, so avoid low-mounted loadouts.

| Placement | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Driver seat | Front | Survives longer; rear seat dies early |
| Guns | Front, mid-height | Clears zombies before they reach you |
| Under-carriage weapons | Avoid | Can cause the build to get stuck |
| Best fuel tank | Front | Rear tanks get destroyed and leak |

## Common Money-Farming Mistakes to Avoid

Even experienced players sabotage their runs with these errors:

- **Overloading on engines.** More engines doesn't mean more distance — it means more weight and faster fuel burn. Two is the community-recommended maximum.
- **Storing fuel in the rear.** Rear fuel barrels are the first casualties of a zombie swarm, and losing them drains your supply faster.
- **Rear-mounted driver seats.** A destroyed seat ends the run no matter how healthy your car is.
- **Weapons mounted under the build.** These can snag and get your car stuck at the start of a drive.
- **Ignoring free codes.** Thousands of Cash sit unclaimed if you haven't checked the code lists recently.
