## Fastest Ways to Earn Tokens Beyond Codes

Your best distance (measured in studs) directly gates certain rewards — the IAMPRO code, for example, requires 1,000 studs, and a past event code required 2,500 studs. Longer runs also mean more kills, more fuel efficiency per zombie destroyed, and bigger payouts.

### 2. Balance Weight Against Firepower

The classic rookie mistake is piling on weapons and extra fuel tanks without accounting for the added weight. A heavily armed but overloaded vehicle crawls along and dies early. Follow this priority framework:

| Priority | Upgrade | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Engine upgrades | Offsets weight from weapons and tanks |
| 2 | One or two strong weapons | Kills hordes efficiently without overloading |
| 3 | Fuel tank(s) | Extends run length |
| 4 | Reinforcement parts | Survivability at higher distances |

### 3. Participate in Every Event

Past events (like the Hacker-themed one) introduced their own token currencies — Lightning Tokens are the current example. History suggests a new event follows each one, so saving leftover tokens and staying active during event windows is one of the smartest long-term plays.

### 4. Use Cash Wisely

Cash and tokens work together. You'll often spin for or unlock Parts and Weapons and then need Cash to actually purchase them. Codes like IAMPRO (5,000x Cash) and COMMUNITY (1,000x Cash plus free parts) keep your wallet ready so tokens never sit idle.

## Common Mistakes That Slow Down Token Progress

Even experienced players leave tokens on the table. Avoid these pitfalls:

- **Ignoring distance requirements**: Token codes with stud requirements can't be redeemed until you've hit the mark — so grinding distance early unlocks future rewards instantly.
- **Overbuilding your vehicle**: More parts isn't always better. Extra weight without engine support kills runs fast.
- **Waiting to redeem codes**: Expired codes like CYBERHACK (2,500x Hacker Tokens) were worth serious value once. Players who hesitated lost out permanently.
- **Skipping the tutorial rush**: You can't redeem anything until the tutorial is done, so knock it out in your first session.
